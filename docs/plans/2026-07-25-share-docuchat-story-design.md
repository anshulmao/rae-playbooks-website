# Share DocuChat feature story

Date: 2026-07-25

## Objective

Reframe the Share feature gallery around DocuChat: a workflow that turns uploaded
documents into trackable, AI-assisted customer experiences while retaining the
existing digital-profile story as the closing slide.

## Sequence

1. **Manage every customer document in one place**
   - Show the DocuChat document dashboard.
   - Explain uploading, organising, sharing, and tracking documents.
   - Surface the dashboard totals for shares, opens, conversations, and actions.
2. **Give customers answers grounded in your content**
   - Show the shared document beside the Rae conversation.
   - Explain that answers are grounded in the shared document and approved
     supporting material such as FAQs and technical product specifications.
   - Position DocuChat as a way for customers to explore information confidently
     without waiting for a seller to answer every question.
3. **Turn customer activity into engagement signals**
   - Show the document activity and analytics view.
   - Explain opens, viewing time, page-level engagement, questions, content gaps,
     conversations, and actions.
   - Connect these signals to better-timed, more relevant customer follow-up.
4. **Present a story worth sharing**
   - Retain the existing completed Digital Profile screenshot and story.
   - Move it to the final position as a broader Share-stage conclusion.

## Content and behavior

Each screenshot retains the established feature-story format: stage eyebrow,
unique headline, fuller supporting paragraph, and a concise “Solution in view”
statement. Existing gallery navigation, thumbnails, counters, and keyboard
behavior remain unchanged.
