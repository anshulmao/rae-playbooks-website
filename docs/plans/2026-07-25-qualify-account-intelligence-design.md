# Qualify account intelligence feature story

Date: 2026-07-25

## Objective

Replace the existing Qualify persona-builder gallery with a four-step account
intelligence story spanning synced personas, curated research, detailed account
insight, and actionable recommendations.

## Entry point

The Qualify stage preview uses the persona dashboard screenshot. Its stage copy
and feature CTA introduce connected persona and account intelligence rather than
the retired Sales Simulator persona-builder experience.

## Sequence

1. **Keep every persona and opportunity in sync**
   - Show the persona-level dashboard.
   - Explain that it brings together stakeholders the user has engaged with,
     their needs, and opportunity context.
   - Explain that Salesforce integration keeps the working view current.
2. **Go deeper on the accounts that matter**
   - Show the curated account-profile research library.
   - Explain how users find relevant account profiles, research, and
     sales-conversation material for target accounts.
3. **Explore the research your way**
   - Show a detailed account-research page.
   - Explain that users can read the complete research or access it
     conversationally through Rae Chat.
4. **Turn research into an account roadmap**
   - Show the actionable recommendations within the research.
   - Explain how engagement approaches, opportunity areas, and next steps help
     users plan the account.

## Content and behavior

Each screenshot uses the existing feature-story format: stage eyebrow, unique
headline, fuller supporting paragraph, and concise “Solution in view” statement.
Existing gallery arrows, thumbnails, counter, and keyboard behavior remain
unchanged.
