The problem

Enterprise sales teams do not lose time in one place.

The friction shows up across the whole deal: searching for product truth, building account context, tailoring messaging, writing the first proposal draft, and preparing for hard questions.

search

Prep is scattered

Account research lives across Google, old decks, internal portals, market notes, and conversations with colleagues.

inventory_2

Product truth moves

Sales teams need current pricing, specs, bundles, and campaign context without waiting for another file to be forwarded.

groups

Every buyer is different

A CFO, IT lead, procurement head, and business owner all need a different pitch for the same solution.

sports_score

Confidence is hard to practise

Reps often enter high-stakes meetings without a safe way to test objections, sharpen answers, or improve delivery.
